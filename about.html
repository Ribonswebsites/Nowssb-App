Questions, answered.

A concise guide to the NowssB experience.

What is NowssB?

NowssB is a digital meditation and wellness experience combining Natural Origin of Word Science, sound practice, daily routines, personal progress, Store content, and NowssB Connect.

What is Natural Origin of Word Science?

It is the central NowssB approach to exploring word origins, sound, meaning, language, and personal practice.

What can I do in NowssB?

You can practice, explore words and meanings, listen to sound-oriented content, save material, build routines, review progress, browse the Store, read guides, personalize the experience, and participate in Connect.

What is NowssB Connect?

It is the social space designed for sharing daily practice, posting a frequency journey, following creators, reacting, and connecting with other practitioners.

What is My Progress?

My Progress is the personal dashboard for sessions, meditation time, streaks, consistency, weekly activity, recent sessions, Body & Mind categories, insights, and feedback.

What is Fashion Plus?

Fashion Plus is the personalization and premium presentation path within NowssB. Exact availability, features, trial terms, and pricing should be checked in the current app or Store.
