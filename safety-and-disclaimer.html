Safety and trust

Use NowssB as a wellness practice, not as medical care.

NowssB is a digital product for personal practice, word and sound exploration, and mindful attention. Clear boundaries help people use it responsibly.

Open NowssBAbout the product
Core disclaimer: NowssB does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition. It is not medical advice and does not replace a qualified healthcare professional.

What the product is designed to support

The experience is designed to support a personal routine involving attention, listening, language curiosity, reflection, and general wellbeing. People may use the app in different ways, and individual experiences will vary.

Do not rely on the app for urgent concerns

If you have a medical or mental-health concern, a sudden change in symptoms, or an urgent situation, contact an appropriately qualified professional or local emergency service. Do not delay care because of something you read or hear in the app.

Be careful with health information

Words, sounds, and personal routines can be meaningful, but they should not be treated as proof of a medical effect. Check important health information against trustworthy clinical or public-health sources.

How to use the experience responsibly

  • Choose a comfortable session length and stop if you feel unwell.
  • Do not use the product while driving or when your attention is needed elsewhere.
  • Keep your personal account details private and use only the current checkout information for purchases.
  • Ask a qualified professional when you need diagnosis, treatment, or personalised medical advice.

Content quality and updates

NowssB may change as the product develops. We aim to make the purpose and limitations of the experience clear, and we encourage users to contact support when something is unclear.
