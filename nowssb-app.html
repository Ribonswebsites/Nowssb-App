The NowssB app

Words, sound, and a practice you can return to.

NowssB is a web app for exploring words, hearing pronunciation, practising at your own pace, and keeping a personal sense of progress. Connect adds a social space for sharing a practice journey with real people.

Open NowssBSee how it works

What you can do

Word Science

Explore the relationship between written form, sound, pronunciation, meaning, and personal attention.

Sound Library

Browse a growing library of words and open the details that help you listen and practise.

Player and practice

Listen, repeat, record your voice, and use the available feedback as an educational practice aid.

Progress

Return to routines and personal history across the devices where you sign in.

Connect

Find public profiles, share posts and Reels, follow people, chat privately, and use voice or video calls when both sessions are available.

Web and Android

Start on nowssb.com. The responsive web experience also supports installation as a PWA and the NowssB Android app builds.

Use it responsibly

NowssB is an educational and wellness-oriented practice experience, not medical care. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or replace professional advice. Read the Safety and wellness disclaimer before using health-related content.
